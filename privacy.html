Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Decider Machine. Your privacy is paramount to us. Because our tools are designed to help you make choices without barriers, we have built this platform to be as private as possible.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal identifying information to use our decision-making tools. We do not "know" who you are when you visit our site.

2. Data Processing (Local Only)

Any data you enter into our tools—such as names in a Spin Wheel, items in a List Picker, or questions for the Oracle—is processed locally on your device. This information is never transmitted to our servers or stored by us.

3. Usage Analytics

We may use anonymous, aggregate analytics to understand how many people visit our site and which tools are most popular. This data does not contain any personal information and is used solely to improve the user experience.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We may use small text files called "cookies" or browser local storage to remember your preferences (like your custom list items) so they are available when you return. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out to us via the contact link on our homepage.
